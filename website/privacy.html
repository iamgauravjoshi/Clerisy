Privacy

Your meetings stay on your computer.

Clerisy is a desktop app. It has no account, no server of its own, and no analytics. This page says plainly what it stores, where, and what it sends to the services you choose.

What Clerisy stores, and where

Everything lives in one folder on your computer, %APPDATA%\Clerisy:

  • Your projects: items, their history, meetings, transcripts, proposed changes and your answers to them, in a local database.
  • Recordings: the audio of your microphone and of the other participants, for as long as you choose to keep it.
  • Settings and API keys. Keys are encrypted with Windows' own data protection, tied to your Windows account.
  • The local speech-to-text model, if you download it.

Uninstalling Clerisy leaves this folder in place, so a reinstall picks up where you were. Delete the folder to remove everything.

What leaves your computer

Only what the services you configure need, and only when you use them:

  • Speech-to-text. With the Local option, audio is transcribed on your computer and never uploaded. With Deepgram, OpenAI or Groq, the recording is sent to that provider to be transcribed.
  • Analysis and Ask. The transcript, your project's items and recent changes are sent to the AI model you chose. With a local Ollama model, that stays on your computer too.
  • Updates. Clerisy checks GitHub for new versions. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Model download. The local speech-to-text model downloads from Hugging Face and GitHub, and each file is checked against a fixed fingerprint.

Those providers' own terms and privacy policies apply to what you send them. Clerisy itself receives nothing.

Recordings and consent

Clerisy records your microphone and your computer's audio, which includes the other people in the call. Recording laws differ by country and state. Tell participants that the meeting is being recorded, and ask them where the law requires it.

How long things are kept

You choose in Settings: keep recordings, delete them once they're transcribed, or delete them after 7 or 30 days. You can also delete one meeting's recording, or all of them, at any time. Transcripts and the project's history are kept until you delete the meeting or the project.
