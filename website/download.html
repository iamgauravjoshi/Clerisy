Clerisy for Windows.

Free, with no account. You'll need an API key for an AI model, or a local Ollama model.

Download the latest version Opens the latest release on GitHub. Download the Clerisy-Setup file.

Requirements

System
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
For local transcription
About 1 GB of free disk space and 2 GB of free memory. A 4-core laptop transcribes a 30-minute meeting in a few minutes.
AI model
An API key for Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter, or a model running in Ollama
Speech-to-text
Local (free, English), or a key for Deepgram, OpenAI or Groq

Installing

Clerisy isn't code-signed yet, so the first time you run the installer, Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. Updates after that install without the warning.

Once it's running, the setup checklist on the first screen walks you through the rest: choose an AI model, choose how meetings are transcribed, and tell Clerisy your name.

Coming from MeetingBuddy?

Clerisy is MeetingBuddy's new name. Installed copies update to Clerisy on their own, and your projects, meetings, recordings and keys come with you.
