How it works

From conversation to project state, in three steps.

1

Capture any meeting, without a bot

Clerisy records your microphone and your computer's audio on your own machine, so it works with whatever app your meeting is in. Nobody else installs anything.

  • Offers to record when a meeting app starts using your microphone
  • Floating controls stay above the call: timer, latest line, mark, stop
  • Stops on its own when the meeting app lets go of the mic
2

Proposals that quote their source

After the meeting, your chosen AI model compares the transcript with the project and proposes what changed. Every proposal must quote the transcript, and Clerisy checks each quote word by word.

  • Requirements, decisions, tasks with owners, deadlines, risks, open questions
  • Flags conflicts with what was agreed in earlier meetings
  • A quote that isn't in the transcript, or drops a "not", is thrown out
3

You approve, the project moves forward

Accept, edit or reject each change. Applying them writes a new version of the project, and every item remembers which meeting changed it, who said so, and the exact words.

  • One board for requirements, decisions, tasks, risks, deadlines and questions
  • A change history, meeting by meeting
  • Share the outcome to Slack, WhatsApp, Markdown or PDF

Ask the project

Questions answered from the record.

Ask "what's still open, and who owns it?" or "why did the launch move?". Clerisy answers from your project's items and meetings only, never from the model's general knowledge.

  • Every point shows the items and quotes behind it
  • Click a quote to open the meeting at that line
  • When the project doesn't say, the answer tells you so

Privacy

Your meetings stay on your machine.

Clerisy is a desktop app, not a service. There's no Clerisy account and no Clerisy server.

Free transcription that never leaves your computer

Local speech-to-text runs offline after a one-time 665 MB download, with speaker labels. English for now; Hindi and Hinglish meetings can go to a cloud provider you choose.

Your own AI model

Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, a local Ollama model, or any OpenAI-compatible server. You pay your provider directly.

Keys stay encrypted

API keys are encrypted with Windows' own data protection, and never shown again, not even to the app's interface.

You decide how long audio lives

Keep recordings, delete them once transcribed, or after 7 or 30 days. Transcripts and decisions stay either way.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. Clerisy records on your own computer, from your microphone and your computer's audio output. Nothing joins the call.

Is it really free?

The app is free, and local transcription is free. The AI analysis uses your own API key, so you pay your AI provider for what you use, or nothing at all with a local Ollama model.

Can the AI make things up?

It can try, which is why every proposed change must quote the transcript, and Clerisy checks each quote word by word. Changes without real evidence are discarded, and nothing reaches your project until you approve it.

Which languages does it support?

Local, offline transcription is English. Hindi and Hinglish meetings work through a cloud provider such as Deepgram, which you can set to handle just those meetings.

Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Clerisy is Windows-only for now, because meeting detection and system-audio capture are Windows-specific.

Do I need to tell people I'm recording?

Yes. Let participants know the meeting is recorded, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
