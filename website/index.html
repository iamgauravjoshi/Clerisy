Why

Not a summary. A record of change.

Meeting notes tell you what people talked about. Then someone still has to work out which decisions moved, which deadlines slipped and which tasks appeared, and copy it all somewhere it lasts. Clerisy does that part, and keeps the receipts.

How it works

Three steps, and you decide the last one.

  1. 01

    Capture, without a bot in the call

    Clerisy records your microphone and your computer's audio on your own machine, so it works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack, Discord or anything else that plays sound. Nobody else has to install anything, and no bot joins the call.

    When a meeting app starts using your microphone, Clerisy offers to record. While it does, a small window stays on top of the call with the timer, the latest line, and buttons to mark a moment or stop.

    The floating controls stay above your meeting app and never take focus from it.

  2. 02

    Proposals that quote their source

    After the meeting, the AI model you chose compares the transcript with your project's current state and proposes changes: new requirements, decisions made or reversed, tasks with owners, moved deadlines, risks, open questions, and conflicts with what was agreed before.

    Each proposal has to quote the transcript, and Clerisy checks every quote word by word. A quote that isn't in the transcript, or one that drops a "not", gets the proposal thrown out before you ever see it.

  3. 03

    You approve, and the project moves forward

    Accept, edit or reject each change. Applying them writes a new version of the project state, and every item keeps its history: which meeting changed it, who said so, and the exact words.

    Months later, "why is the launch on the 20th?" has an answer you can click through to.

Ask the project

Questions answered from the record.

Ask "what's still open, and who owns it?" or "why did the launch move?", and Clerisy answers from your project's items and meetings only, never from the model's general knowledge.

Every point in the answer shows the items and quotes behind it. Points that nothing backs up are left out, and when the project simply doesn't say, the answer tells you so.

Privacy

Yours, on your machine.

Clerisy is a desktop app, not a service. There's no Clerisy account and no Clerisy server.

Free transcription that never leaves your computer
Local speech-to-text runs offline after a one-time 665 MB download, with speaker labels. English for now; Hindi and Hinglish meetings can go to a cloud provider you choose.
Your own AI model
Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, a local Ollama model, or any OpenAI-compatible server. You pay your provider directly.
Keys stay encrypted
API keys are encrypted with Windows' own data protection and never shown again, not even to the app's interface.
You decide how long audio lives
Keep recordings, delete them once transcribed, or after 7 or 30 days. Transcripts and decisions stay either way.

Also

The details that make it usable every week.

Speaker labels you can fix
Tell Clerisy how many people were in the meeting, merge voices it split, and give them names.
Share the outcome
Copy the approved changes as a Slack or WhatsApp message or as Markdown, or save a PDF.
Global hotkeys
Ctrl+Shift+M starts and stops recording; Ctrl+Shift+D marks a moment the AI should weigh more.
Import what you already have
Transcripts from Zoom, Teams (VTT/SRT) or plain text, and audio or video files.
Change history
Every applied meeting is a version of the project, with a before-and-after for each item.
Updates itself
New versions download in the background and install when you restart, never in the middle of a recording.
